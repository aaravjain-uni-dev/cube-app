2x2 Cube

This is a pocket-sized puzzle with over 3.6 million possible positions.

What is a 2x2 Cube?

The 2x2 Rubik's Cube is a smaller version of the classic 3x3 cube. It has eight corner pieces and no visible edge or centre pieces. Although it may look simple, the 2x2 has millions of possible positions and can be solved using several different methods.

Quick facts about the 2x2 cube

Pieces

It contains exactly 8 corner pieces and no center or edge pieces.

Combinations

It has 3,674,160 unique possible states (even though it has total 8 pieces!)

Max moves

Any scrambled state, when solved with the most efficient turns, requires at most 11 moves. This is known as "God's Number". However, a human may take more than 11 moves to solve it.

Invention

The 2x2 cube was originally patented by Larry D. Nichols in 1972 using magnets. However, the modern version we use today was designed by Ernő Rubik and mass-produced starting in 1981.

World Record

The single fastest solve is 0.39 seconds, set by Ziyu Ye in 2025.
