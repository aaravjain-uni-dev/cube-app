3x3 Cube

This is the iconic original puzzle with over 43 quintillion possible positions.

What is a 3x3 Cube?

The 3x3 Rubik's Cube is the world's most famous combination puzzle. It features a central core, six fixed center pieces, twelve edge pieces, and eight corner pieces. Invented as a teaching tool, it has grown into a global phenomenon solved using a variety of beginner and advanced methods.

Quick facts about the 3x3 cube

Pieces

It contains 26 external pieces: 6 fixed center pieces, 12 edge pieces, and 8 corner pieces.

Combinations

It has 43,252,003,274,489,856,000 (over 43 quintillion) unique possible states.

Max moves

Any scrambled state, when solved with the most efficient turns, requires at most 20 moves. This is known as "God's Number". However, a human may take more than 20 moves to solve it.

Invention

Invented in 1974 by Hungarian architecture professor Ernő Rubik, it was originally named the "Magic Cube" before being rebranded and sold worldwide in 1980.

World Record

The single fastest solve for a standard 3x3 cube is 2.76 seconds, set by Teodor Zajderk in 2023.
